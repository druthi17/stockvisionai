How the Prediction Works

Understand the 6-stage end-to-end Machine Learning Pipeline

1

Data Fetching

5 years of daily historical data (Open, High, Low, Close, Volume) downloaded via yfinance.

2

Feature Scaling

MinMaxScaler scales features to [0, 1] fitted strictly on training data to prevent leakage.

3

Sequence Building

Sliding window of 60 trading days maps temporal patterns to predict day 61 closing price.

4

LSTM Network

2 stacked LSTM layers (64 & 32 units) capture non-linear temporal dynamics and trends.

5

Inverse Transform

Predicted value is converted from [0, 1] scale back into native currency price.

6

Smart Caching

Models and scalers are cached on disk to avoid redundant retraining within 3 days.
